Who it's for

For anyone who wants to learn boogie-woogie and blues piano from scratch, calmly and without complicated sheet music — at any age. If you have a keyboard (even a simple one) and a computer, you have everything you need. The exercises start from the very first touch and lead you, step by step, to playing a whole tune.

Every exercise has the two hands already on separate tracks, a file to play and an audio preview to hear how it sounds. Plus a guide and a step-by-step learning path.

How it works

  • Download the course and one of the apps suggested below.
  • Open an exercise: you see the notes fall, one row for the right hand and one for the left.
  • With wait mode, the notes wait for you: you never fall behind.
  • Practise one hand at a time, slowly. Speed comes on its own.

Which app to play with

The course files are in MIDI format. You need an app that shows them as “falling notes”. Pick the one you prefer:

SynthesiaRecommended. Download (PC, Mac, tablet). The one the course is built for: separate hands and wait mode.
NeothesiaFreeDownload, PC/Mac/Linux. Falling notes.
PianoFall / MidianoFreeIn the browser, nothing to install.
MuseScore 4FreePlays the files and shows the score.

App prices and versions may change: always check each app's official website.
